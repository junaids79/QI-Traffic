OBJECTIVE FUNCTION

Multi-objective route optimization

min F = wtT + wdD + wcC + wlPdeadline

The engine combines travel time, distance, accumulated congestion, and deadline penalty. Weights are user-configurable and the selected route is reported with measured fitness.

CONSTRAINT MODEL

Feasibility conditions

ACADEMIC NOTE

Quantum-inspired, not quantum hardware

QPSO is implemented as a stochastic discrete route search on classical computing infrastructure. Dijkstra remains a deterministic shortest-path reference and A* accelerates search with a geographic heuristic. No claim is made that QPSO is universally superior; the benchmark page reports measured trade-offs.
